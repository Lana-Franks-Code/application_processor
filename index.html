What the processor does
Step 01
Merge Employment Data
Joins headcount from Sheet 2 into Summary Data by Application ID. Flags unmatched rows.
Step 02
Remove Duplicate Columns
Deletes duplicate Full Name and Email columns left by the Accelerator export.
Step 03
Age Validation
Calculates age from Date of Birth. Flags under 18 or over 49 in red.
Step 04
Contact Deduplication
Clears Alternative Contact if it's identical to the primary number.
Step 05
Primary Industry
Consolidates split Primary Industry columns — fills gaps then removes the duplicate.
Step 06
Basic Compliance
Red-flags applicants missing CIPC registration, business bank account, or tax clearance.
Step 07
Years in Operation
Calculates business age in years from the Business Registration Date.
Step 08
Revenue Deselection
Applies revenue minimums by business age. IQR outlier detection on latest year. Deselects below threshold.
Step 09
Employment Sense-Check
Flags implausible headcount-to-revenue ratios, wage bill ceilings, and hard FT/total limits.
